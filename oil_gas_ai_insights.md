## AI in Oil and Gas Industry - Key Insights from DNV

**Market Growth:** The global market for AI applications in oil and gas is projected to reach nearly USD 3 billion in 2024 and USD 5.2 billion in 2029.

**Current Adoption:** While 47% of oil and gas professionals anticipate using AI in operations in 2024, only 15% are currently using it in live, day-to-day operations. A significant portion (47%) are in planning or piloting stages.

**Trust and Assurance:** A major barrier to wider adoption is the need for assurance that AI can deliver value effectively, efficiently, securely, and safely. External developers and suppliers need to prove trustworthiness through validation and verification.

**Opportunities/Use Cases:**
*   **Efficiency and Sustainability:** Boosting efficiency in oil exploration and enhancing sustainability in managing complex development projects.
*   **Predictive Maintenance:** Optimizing predictive maintenance of assets like oil platforms, pipelines, and refineries to avoid downtime and enhance safety. This includes applications in non-destructive testing (NDT) and mooring line reliability.
*   **Environmental Monitoring:** Identifying and quantifying oil spills and methane releases using data from sensors, aerial, and satellite cameras.
*   **Business Optimization:** Improving demand forecasting, managing price fluctuations, assisting with regulatory compliance, and driving supply-chain efficiency.
*   **Digital Twins:** AI can enhance digital twin capabilities for simulation-based models of assets, providing real-time insights and decision support.

**Risks Associated with AI:**
*   **Data Quality:** Incomplete, inaccurate, or limited data can lead to erroneous insights and flawed decisions.
*   **Cybersecurity:** Connecting AI/ML to critical control systems creates potential attack surfaces.
*   **Regulatory Risk:** Potential for contravening laws on privacy, transparency, cybersecurity, and safety.
*   **Skill Shortage:** A global shortage of professionals who understand both AI/ML technology and the specific oil and gas systems.

**Managing AI Risk:**
*   Data quality management.
*   Validation and verification of AI/ML products and services.
*   Cybersecurity monitoring, testing, and updating.
*   Compliance with evolving regulations (e.g., EU AI Act).
*   Training personnel on AI/ML limitations and reliable use.

**Strategic Importance:** Front-runner companies are integrating AI Strategy into their top-level management frameworks alongside Digitalization Strategy and Data Strategy.
