# Features

## Authoritative List of What the Project Can Do

### Core Site
- **Homepage** – Frontier AI solutions, morphing hero text, Austin collaboration, Hylios teaser
- **Navbar** – Home, Ethereal Search, Austin, Hylios, Partner with Us
- **Contact** – Modal form with Resend + Supabase integration
- **Mobile** – Responsive layout, hamburger menu

### Ethereal Search (Feature-Flagged)
- **Hub** (`/ethereal-search`) – Coming soon placeholder; when `NEXT_PUBLIC_ETHEREAL_SEARCH_FLOWS_ENABLED=true`, shows links to Explorer + Logo Lab
- **EtherealExplorer** (`/ethereal-search/explorer`) – Full product: hero, document gallery, feature cards, agentic demo, pricing
- **Logo Lab** (`/ethereal-search/concepts`) – 12 background themes (constellation, vector, quantum, sand, rain, aurora, neural, nebula, fluid, firefly, waves, crystalline) with unified controls
- **Animation Library** (`/animations`) – 50 standalone themed animations in 5 categories, 6 color themes

### Austin AI
- **Austin Page** (`/austin-ai`) – Barricade detection demos, capabilities, Hylios teaser

### Hylios
- **Hylios Page** (`/hylios`) – Magical Space Intelligence, AR + ML room scanner

### Technical
- **Feature Flag** – `NEXT_PUBLIC_ETHEREAL_SEARCH_FLOWS_ENABLED` toggles Explorer + Logo Lab visibility
- **Document Images** – 8 distinct images from v0-ethereal-explorer for document cards
- **Logo** – Ethereal Dimension wireframe cube at `/assets/ethereal-logo.png`
