# Ethereal Dimension — Site (PRODUCT.md)

## What this is
The marketing site for **Ethereal Dimension**, a frontier-AI company deploying computer vision, AR, and
spatial intelligence into the built environment (cities, infrastructure, construction).

## Audience
- **Primary:** city/public-agency decision makers (e.g. Austin DoT), infrastructure teams, and
  engineering partners evaluating AI vendors.
- **Secondary:** iOS consumers discovering **Hylios** (AR + ML room scanner), and investors/partners
  evaluating the company.
- Visitor mode: **Persuade.** The visitor decides and acts — distinctive type, committed palette,
  image-led heroes, one clear action per section.

## What the page must prove
1. **We deploy, not demo** — real city deployments (Austin DoT barricade detection) with concrete
   numbers (97.2% accuracy, 60% review time saved, 24/7 monitoring).
2. **Products exist** — Hylios is on the App Store; Ethereal Search is in preview.
3. **Credibility for the built-environment buyer** — restrained, technical, trustworthy tone.

## Structure
- **Nav:** Work · Hylios · Ethereal Search · Company + "Partner with us" pill
- **Hero:** eyebrow, serif display headline, sub, two CTAs, stats row
- **Work:** two deployment cards (Austin DoT barricade detection, street-level vision)
- **Hylios:** product section with scan visual + App Store CTA
- **Ethereal Search:** dedicated page (preview)
- **CTA:** "Ready to transform your future?" → mailto
- **Footer:** copyright + contact

## Non-goals (v1)
- No booking/CRM flows on this site (Antara marketplace is a separate product).
- No blog, no pricing page.

## Deploy
- Next.js (App Router) on Vercel. Domain: etherealdimension.io.
