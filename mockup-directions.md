# Ethereal Search: Visual Directions

These concept visuals are for choosing a direction before any further implementation. All three use the existing Ethereal Dimension identity: deep ink surfaces, editorial type, thin evidence rules, and a single electric-teal accent.

| Direction | Visual thesis | Interaction focus | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Evidence Desk** | An answer and its evidence occupy the same calm workbench. | A source-aware query with a visible answer and citation trail. | The clearest first product story and the recommended lead. |
| **Project Atlas** | The project record becomes a navigable place rather than a pile of files. | Collection-first exploration across plans, field records, constraints, and permits. | Teams that begin with a known corridor, site, or document set. |
| **Field Thread** | A search becomes a traceable decision path. | Supporting and conflicting evidence progress toward an explicit next step. | Review workflows where accountability matters more than speed alone. |

## Recommendation

Begin with **Evidence Desk**. It is the most legible proof that Ethereal Search is not merely generating text: the visitor can immediately see the query, grounded conclusion, and evidence trail together. Project Atlas and Field Thread are strong secondary surfaces once users are inside the product.
