## Decision criteria

The work should make Ethereal Search feel like a present, credible product without inventing deployment claims, keep the customer journey on the main site, and follow the existing restrained dark-editorial identity. The design must avoid generic AI-dashboard conventions, excessive card nesting, decorative glows, and preview-only calls to action.
